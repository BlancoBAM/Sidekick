Capture instructions
Choose the Tab containing your course video, then enable Also share tab audio. Do not select Window or Entire screen. The first browser run downloads the Whisper model (~200 MB); wait for browser whisper: ready before playback.
ELI10 — Step 1
  1. Click Start sharing.
  2. Your browser will pop up a box. Pick the Tab that has your course video.
  3. Tick the box that says Also share tab audio. Do not pick "Window" or "Entire screen" — only a Tab sends the video's sound.
  4. Wait for the status line to say browser whisper: ready (first time downloads ~200 MB).
  5. Play the video. Words will start appearing in the Live transcript panel on the right.

Tab audio captures the sound produced by the selected tab, not your microphone or other desktop audio.

STEP 3

Ask Sidekick

Transcript intelligence
ELI10 — Step 3
  1. Paste your quiz question and its options (A), B), C)…) into the box.
  2. Click Analyze question.
  3. Sidekick reads its transcript notes and answers with: Answer, Why (with a timestamp like [02:15]), and Confidence.

HIGH confidence indicates strong transcript support. LOW confidence means the video may not cover the question; verify before submitting.
